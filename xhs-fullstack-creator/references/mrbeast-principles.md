# MrBeast Content Science (v1.0)

Based on leaked internal training materials, 6 deep podcasts, and external analysis.

## Core Mental Models

### 1. CTR × AVD Dual Engine
```
Traffic Recommendation ∝ CTR × AVD × Engagement Rate

Key insights:
- High CTR gets initial click
- High AVD keeps algorithm recommending
- Both must be strong for viral growth
```

### 2. Zero-Boring-Moments Principle
Every second must earn its place. If a viewer could leave without missing anything, cut it.

### 3. The 30-Second Rule
Your first 30 seconds determine 80% of success rate. Structure:
- 0-3s: Visual shock or counter-intuitive statement
- 3-10s: Promise stakes or consequence
- 10-30s: Deliver promise partially (keep them wanting more)

## Staircase Progression Formula

```
P1-P2: Hook → Suspense建立
   ↓
P3-P4: Value delivery (core content)
   ↓  
P5: Twist/Reversal (new information)
   ↓
P6: Call-to-action (set up next episode)
```

Each "step" should deliver a mini-satisfaction before moving to the next tension point.

## Title/Thumbnail Formulas

### High-CTR Title Patterns:
1. **Number-based**: "I Spent $10,000 On..." / "7 Mistakes That Cost Me..."
2. **Contrast-based**: "Before vs After" / "What I Expected vs Reality"
3. **Question-based**: "Is This Legal?" / "Why Does Everyone Hate This?"
4. **Secret-based**: "The Truth About..." / "No One Tells You This..."

### Thumbnail Best Practices:
- Max 3 elements maximum (face + object + text)
- High contrast colors (yellow/black, white/red work best)
- Exaggerated facial expressions
- Before/after split layouts
- Big numbers visible at small size

## Pacing Rules

1. **Change every 3-4 seconds** - visual, audio, or information
2. **Mini-cliffhangers every 30-45 seconds** - tease next segment
3. **Pattern interrupts** - unexpected cuts, zoom-ins, sound effects
4. **Never explain twice** - if you said it, move forward

## Engagement Triggers

### Comments (higher priority than likes):
- Ask specific questions ("Which mistake would you make?")
- Create controversy ("This controversial opinion...")
- Invite corrections ("Did I miss anything?")

### Saves (high value signal):
- Checklists ("Save this before your renovation")
- Templates ("Download my budget sheet")
- Reference guides ("Pin this for later")

### Shares (viral potential):
- Relatability triggers ("Tag someone who needs this")
- Status signaling ("Only 1% know this trick")
- Utility value ("Send this to a friend renovating")
